Common questions

I'm stuck at almost 100% clean. Tap Find dirt: the car turns to the dirtiest side and leftover mud glows orange. Once the car is nearly clean you can also tap Hand back the keys.

How do I get more coins? Finish jobs, the daily challenge and the weekly monster truck, or watch an optional short video for free coins (up to 5 a day).

Does the game need the internet? No. Only the optional videos need a connection.
