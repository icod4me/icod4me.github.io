Mud Busters Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Mud Busters is a car-wash game for iPhone, iPad and Android, made by icod4me. This page explains what information the game uses. In short: the game itself collects nothing about you. The optional reward videos are provided by Google AdMob, which receives limited technical data to show family-safe ads.

What the game stores

Your progress (coins, cars, garage, settings) is saved only on your device. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no in-app purchases. We do not collect, receive or sell any personal information, and we have no servers that receive your game data.

Reward videos (Google AdMob)

The game shows a video only when the player chooses to watch one, for example to earn free coins or to double the pay for a job. There are no banner ads and no ads that interrupt play. These videos are served by Google AdMob. To show and measure an ad, Google receives technical information such as your device type, operating system, approximate location from your IP address, and whether the ad was watched or tapped.

Every ad request from Mud Busters is marked as directed at children, limited to G-rated (general audience) ads and non-personalised. The app does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA or Android advertising ID) and never asks for permission to track you.

Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Notifications

If you allow them, the game shows friendly reminders (for example when your daily gift is ready). They are scheduled on your device; nothing is sent to us.

Children

Mud Busters is designed for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are optional, family-safe and not personalised. Parents can stop ads at any time by turning off the device's network connection; the game works fully offline.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes all of its data. You can also reset progress in the game's Settings.
