Common questions

How do I drive? Drag the steering wheel on the left. Hold Gas and Brake on the right; hold Brake when stopped to reverse. Follow the yellow GPS line and stop beside the bus shelter to open the doors.

How do I play sideways? Tap TURN while driving, or choose Settings › Screen.

A "Watch a video" button says "Loading a video…" Videos need an internet connection and sometimes none are available. Try again in a minute; the game works fine without them.

How do I start again? Settings › Reset progress. Your progress is saved only on your device.
