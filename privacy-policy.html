City Express Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

City Express is a driving game for iPhone, iPad and Android. This page explains what information the game uses. In short: the game itself collects nothing about you. The optional reward videos are provided by Google AdMob, which receives limited technical data to show family-safe ads.

What the game stores

Your progress (coins, stars, unlocked vehicles, settings) is saved only on your device. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no in-app purchases. We do not collect, receive or sell any personal information, and we have no servers that receive your game data.

Reward videos (Google AdMob)

The game shows a video only when the player chooses to watch one, for example to earn free coins or a free repair. These videos are served by Google AdMob. To show and measure an ad, Google receives technical information such as your device type, operating system, approximate location from your IP address, and whether the ad was watched or tapped.

Every ad request from City Express is marked as directed at children and limited to G-rated (general audience) ads. With these settings Google does not show personalised ads and the app does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA or Android advertising ID); the app never asks for permission to track you.

Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

City Express is designed to be suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are optional, family-safe and not personalised. Parents can stop ads being shown at any time by turning off the device's network connection while playing.

Deleting your data

Because your progress is stored only on your device, deleting the app removes it. You can also use Settings › Reset progress inside the game.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
