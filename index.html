CITY EXPRESS

Drive the city bus. Run missions. Outrun the police.

City Express is a 3D driving game for iPhone and Android. Drive a full-size city bus, a taxi, an ambulance or a sports car through a living city with traffic lights, pedestrians, rain, snow and a real day and night cycle.

  • Pick up passengers at bus stops, finish timed routes and earn up to three stars
  • Six camera views, including behind the steering wheel
  • Drive badly and the police chase you, helicopter included
  • Family-friendly: no accounts, no chat, no in-app purchases; only optional reward videos
